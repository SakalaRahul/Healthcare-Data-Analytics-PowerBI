# Analytical Insights & Strategic Recommendations

Based on the quantitative analysis of the 12,000+ healthcare appointment records from 2022 to 2025:

## Key Findings:
1. **Revenue Drivers:** Oncology and Cardiology departments generated the highest cumulative revenue, contributing over 35% of total hospital income due to higher specialized consultation fees.
2. **Operational Bottleneck:** Orthopedics and General Medicine exhibited the longest average patient waiting times (34.2 minutes), exceeding the hospital's target SLA threshold of 30 minutes.
3. **No-Show Patterns:** The overall No-Show rate averaged ~12.5%. Mondays and Fridays registered a 4.2% higher no-show rate compared to mid-week days.
4. **Doctor Efficiency:** Senior physicians (>15 years experience) maintained high consultation completion rates (94%) but experienced 18% longer patient wait times due to complex case loads.

## Strategic Recommendations:
- **Overbooking Adjustments:** Implement dynamic booking slots on Mondays/Fridays to mitigate the revenue impact of no-shows.
- **Queue Optimization:** Deploy digital check-in kiosks in Orthopedics to reduce consultation wait times by an estimated 15%.
- **SLA Incentives:** Tie doctor performance scores to waiting time SLA achievement metrics.
